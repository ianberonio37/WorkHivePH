# ── AI crawlers ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Explicit allow signals intent and helps AEO/GEO citation eligibility. Each vendor
# runs SEPARATE tokens for training vs retrieval vs user-initiated fetch, and they do
# NOT inherit from one another — a directive for ClaudeBot says nothing about
# Claude-SearchBot. The retrieval/user tokens are the ones that produce CITATIONS.
# Every bot is allowed deliberately: a blocked bot forfeits an estimated 18-34% of
# that engine's potential citations.
#
# WHY EVERY GROUP REPEATS THE DISALLOWS (RFC 9309): a crawler obeys the group matching
# its product token, and falls back to "*" ONLY when no group matches it. So a named
# group containing just "Allow: /" does not inherit the "*" group's Disallow lines — it
# silently re-opens them. Naming these bots without repeating the rules exposed the
# stale backup copies of the homepage to precisely the engines we want citing the live
# one. Add a bot here => copy the Disallow block with it.

#   OAI-AdsBot    = validates pages submitted as ChatGPT ads; NOT used for training
