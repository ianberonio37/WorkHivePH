## Priority Issues (fixed in this walk, verified live)
- [P1] **"Healthy" over eight slow cards.** The rollup counted a slow answer as up - right for availability - and called
  it healthy. It now says "10/10 services answering (100%) · 8 slower than the 2 s target". Verified in the cold-start
  scenario on a throwaway page (ai-gateway hanging, platform-gateway 503): "8/9 services answering (89%) · 8 slower than
  the 2 s target · 1 DOWN · 1 did not answer in time (not counted either way)" - the 8/9, 1 DOWN and "did not answer"
  that tools/prove_status_cold_is_not_down.mjs checks all hold.
- [P2] **A stale layout reserve stretched every card.** The grid's 1148px min-height was measured for one column at 390
  and two at 1280, but 1280 lays out four columns, and grid rows stretch to fill a min-height. Rows now take their
  content's height and the reserve is per column band, measured with the probes settled: 1160 / 700 / 480 / 420 / 320 for
  1 to 5 columns (content 1128 / 678 / 444 / 390 / 288). At 1280 the grid is 420px instead of 1148.
- [P2] **Ten cards named in the code's words.** Each card now leads with what the service does - AI assistant, Platform
  services, Knowledge search, Voice commands, Voice reports, Hive memory, Analytics briefs, Asset Brain, Search
  indexing, Data imports (from each function's own header) - and keeps the function id on its status line. The status
  prover now finds a card by its data-fn.
- [P3] **The subtitle** reads "Live checks of the services behind WorkHive, each measured against the targets at the
  bottom of this page."
