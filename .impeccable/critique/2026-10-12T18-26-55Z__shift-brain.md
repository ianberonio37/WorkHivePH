## Design Specificity Verdict
A handover board built around how shifts actually run: risk, PMs, jobs carried over with their rule stated, parts to
pre-stage, a draft a supervisor publishes. It already knew a stale plan is dangerous (F15); the rest of the page had
not caught up with the badge.

Deterministic scan (Assessment B): detector clean - no claim needed. Walk: 0 overlap, 0 controls under 44 px, 0
low-contrast readings, smallest text 12 px, 0 heading skips, 0 nested cards, 0 layout motion, no spill, at both widths;
theme 11 of 11 readers on the tokens.

## What's Working
- With no model answer the plan is honest: "No written summary for this shift: the AI assistant did not answer, so this
  plan carries the counts only", signed and grounded.
- The source line distinguishes "generated on load" from "loaded from storage" from STALE.
- Carry-forward states its rule instead of an adjective.
