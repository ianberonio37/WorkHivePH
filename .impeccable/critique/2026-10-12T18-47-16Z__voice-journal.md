## Design Specificity Verdict
A private voice notebook for a plant floor: Hala-flavoured replies, a typed fallback for a bay too loud to speak in, a
privacy promise read off the RLS policies, and an on-device voice option whose audio never leaves the plant.

Deterministic scan (Assessment B): detector clean - no claim needed. Walk: 0 overlap, 0 controls under 44 px (1 before:
the inline data-rights email link, 154x15), 0 low-contrast readings, smallest text 12 px, 0 heading skips, 0 layout
motion, no spill, at both widths; theme 6 of 6 readers on the tokens. The 11 "nested cards" are the type-instead
disclosure (a .wh-help panel, kept by a recorded decision - "a disclosure, not a second hero") and its fields.

## What's Working
- The typed fallback exists and is named, for every worker who cannot speak aloud.
- Privacy is stated where the writing happens, worded from the actual policies.
- Each reply is attached to its question, with "Speak again" beside it.

## Priority Issues (fixed in this walk, verified live)
- [P2] **The voice toggle named one thing and announced another.** Pressed means on-device voice; the off state was
  labelled "Cloud voice", so a screen reader heard "Cloud voice, toggle button, not pressed" while the cloud voice was
  speaking. The label now reads "Local voice: off" / "Local voice: on", so the words and aria-pressed agree and the
  visible text stays inside the accessible name. Verified: "Local voice: off / pressed=false".
- [P3] **A 14px microphone in a 132px disc** - now 44px tall (60x44), so the page's main control shows what it does.
- [P3] **The source line** is placed under the 42rem column instead of at the viewport's left edge (verified: its left
  edge matches the column's at 1280 and 390).
- [P3] **The data-rights email link** has a 45px hit area (vertical padding on the inline box; the line does not move).

## Persona Red Flags
- **Screen-reader user**: before this change, the one control that decides whether a recording's reply is synthesized
  on-device or in the cloud was announced backwards in its default state.

## Minor Observations
- Two Back controls: the shared band at the top-left and the page header's own "Back". The page has an embedded mode,
  so the header's is left in place pending a look at how it behaves when embedded.
- The journal shows eight consecutive entries of the same question about an unregistered asset (seeded data).
