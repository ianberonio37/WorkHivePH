## Design Specificity Verdict
Authored for this product: the greeting names the plant's day and shift ("Tuesday, Oct 13 - Night Shift (PHT)"), the
critical line names an asset and its MTBF with the page that resolves it, the four tiles are the plant's own counts with
their sources stated, and the companion the person chose (Zaniah) is on the first screen.

Deterministic scan (Assessment B): nested-cards 8, clipped-overflow-container 9, dark-glow 2, cramped-padding 1 - the
signed-in claim reads true today and is carried. Walk: 0 overlap, 0 controls under 44 px, 0 low-contrast readings,
smallest text 12 px, 0 heading skips, no spill, at both widths; off-token colour reads 32 -> 1 (#ops-home's own near-navy
background). The cursor glow still registers at 390 because the walker emulates a narrow DESKTOP; a real touch screen
matches the new coarse-pointer rule.

## What's Working
- One verdict line and one destination: "TT-001: risk 86% - MTBF 13d. Inspect now - Open Asset Hub".
- The consent card asks before anything is measured, and the default is denied while it waits.
- The home is short: four tiles, two actions, the rest behind More.

## Priority Issues (all fixed in this walk, verified live)
- [P1] **The analytics card's heading sat under the fixed nav.** wh-consent.js moves the card to the top of <main>, which
  carries no top padding (each section clears the nav for itself), so "Analytics on this page" (top 17) and its first
  line (top 45) were painted under the nav (bottom 65) - on the signed-in home and the public landing, at both widths.
  David was asked to Allow or decline below a sentence whose beginning he could not read. The card now clears whatever is
  pinned across the top; the section after it drops its now-redundant nav padding while the card shows. Measured: heading
  at 90, clear, at both widths in both states; the greeting 52 px under the card; the sign-in wall's username, sign-in
  and SSO controls clear at 390; the hub button clear. (/impeccable harden)
- [P2] **Two shift models.** The home's line used its own boundaries - Morning before 12, Afternoon to 17, then
  "Evening" - while shift-brain plans by 06-14 Morning, 14-22 Afternoon, 22-06 Night. At 00:13 PHT the home said "Morning
  Shift" during the night shift; at 13:00 and 18:00 the two pages disagreed again. Now shift-brain's windows: "Night Shift
  (PHT)" at 00:13.
- [P2] **32 off-token colour reads.** The open/high badges tinted with Tailwind orange-400, the Voice Journal action with
  rose-400, and the shared severity badge (whOhBadge in utils.js - this home, shift-brain, alert-hub, and the stray green
  on hive) with Tailwind -300 tints, critical's #FECACA one unit from --wh-red-text. Each is now its token: red-text,
  orange-text, amber, green over their own channels. 32 -> 1 read.
- [P2] **"A new version is ready" was painted as an error**, in the red the platform keeps for failures, on every page
  after every deploy - and its "ref" stamped the DEVICE's time (05:22 on this New Zealand-time host while the logs said
  00:22 PHT), defeating its purpose of matching a report to the logs. The update notice is navy and blue now (failures
  keep their red), and every notice's ref reads "00:24:23 PHT". (/impeccable clarify)
- [P3] **The header controls wrapped into ragged right-aligned rows** under a left-aligned greeting (three rows at 390).
  Start-aligned now: one block under the greeting at both widths.
- [P3] **The cursor glow was a 440 px composited layer on every phone** - it follows a mouse and is never moved on touch.
  Hidden on coarse pointers.
