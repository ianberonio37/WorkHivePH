## What's Working
- The verdict counts areas, says so, and keeps the real figures underneath it.

## Priority Issues (all fixed in this walk, verified live)
- [P1] **The board ran on the device's clock.** The eyebrow, the greeting and "Jobs logged today" read the device: on a
  device set to Auckland the board said "Tuesday morning" while the hive's day was Monday 23:56 PHT, and "since midnight"
  began at Monday 19:00 PHT. The platform promises one clock (whFmtDate pins Asia/Manila; migration 20261009000066), so
  the hour, weekday and midnight are now the hive's. Verified on a device set to New York at Monday 12:07: "Hive Board -
  Tuesday morning" (PHT 00:07) and "jobs today" counted from 2026-10-12T16:00Z, the Philippine midnight.
- [P1] **The "What the hive has flagged" card rendered in fragments** - since it was added on 2026-09-11. Its style named
  align-items and gap and never display:flex, so an inline <a> styled as a card wrapped block content and broke apart: a
  17 px rounded sliver beside the title, the subtitle spilling across its edge, "Open Alert Hub" in a box of its own. Now a
  flex row: one box, 87 px at 390 and 67 px at 1280.
- [P2] **The brief misreported its age and lost its source.** "Today's Brief" and "refreshed daily" sat over "Updated 7d
  ago" - and the age was the FIRST report type found, while the newest report was 20 hours old. On every reload inside the
  8 h cache the provenance chip vanished, because it rendered after the cache's early return. Now "Latest brief", the
  newest report's age, a chip that says when it last refreshed once that is more than a day and a half ago, and the chip
  cached with the bullets (verified on a fresh and a cached load).
- [P2] **The adoption copy talked about the reader in the third person.** On a two-member hive the champion candidate is
  Gloria, so What to do next said "Pair Gloria Tan with whoever's slipping" - to Gloria, who is herself 12 days quiet. When
  the champion is the reader, the next step, the healthy summary and the Why? drawer now say "you" (a display-name match
  that only picks the wording).
- [P2] **The danger and success tints were Tailwind's**, 239,68,68 and 34,197,94 beside --wh-red-text and --wh-green
  text, at 26 sites (badges, alerts, buttons, success circles). Now the tokens' channels at the same alphas.
- [P3] **The readiness bar animated `width`**, and its note kept it on the belief that only scaling squeezes the gradient -
  measured at 40/100 it already ended in full green. A full-width gradient clipped from the right now shows where on the
  ramp the hive stands, and repaints without reflowing the card. Layout motion 1 -> 0.
- [P3] **46 px of dead air under "Live data"**: components.css reserves 62 px on every .wh-source-chip and this one is one
  line. The container now reserves what it renders, 66 px at both widths.
