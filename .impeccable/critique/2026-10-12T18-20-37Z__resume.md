## Design Specificity Verdict
Built for this audience: TESDA NC levels beside skill levels, PRC licences, OFW applications named as a reason to keep
separate resumes, photo-per-page upload for a paper CV, and a fabrication rail that keeps the AI from inventing metrics.

Deterministic scan (Assessment B): detector clean - no claim needed. Walk: 0 overlap, 0 controls under 44 px, 0
low-contrast readings, smallest text 12 px, 0 heading skips, 0 layout motion, no spill, at both widths; theme 8 of 8
readers on the tokens after the fix (3 page-local greys before). The 7 "nested cards" are the details form's inputs inside
their card - bordered fields, not cards. `tools/validate_resume.py` 78/78 (it was 75/78: two checks matched a renamed
variable and an old 24px literal, so they failed on correct code; both now test the contract, teeth-tested).

## What's Working
- The page says what each door does before it is opened: files are read and discarded, nothing is added until Confirm.
- A failed read is never shown as an empty resume.
- The AI helpers stay hidden until there is something to work on.

## Priority Issues (fixed in this walk, verified live)
- [P2] **Examples that read as his own data.** Headline "Mechanical Maintenance Technician", city "Batangas City", region
  "Batangas" were placeholders at 72% white beside values at 96% - real enough to read as filled fields, and an exported
  resume would then go out without them. Examples are now marked: "e.g. Maintenance Technician", "e.g. Batangas City",
  "e.g. Batangas", and the item editors' examples the same way; email and phone keep their format placeholders.
- [P2] **An Undo that looked ready with nothing to undo.** It now looks idle while its stack is empty (aria-disabled, so it
  stays focusable and still answers "Nothing to undo."), with a tooltip naming what it steps back over, and wakes when a
  step is pushed. Verified through the page's own controls: idle on the blank resume, still idle after adding a skill
  (adds were never undoable), awake after removing it; Ben has no cloud resume, so the removal snapshot wrote nothing.
- [P3] **Page-local greys** #9AA8B6 (intro and source line), #9fb0c0 (start card, review sheet, resume manager, item
  labels) and #b6c4d1 now use --wh-text-muted, brighter than all three.

## Persona Red Flags
- **First-time CV writer on a phone**: the start card that names the three ways in sits below the details form, after
  the two controls it describes.

## Minor Observations
- The "Promote, don't duplicate" checkbox renders as a 44px native box, kept at that size because the walk measures the
  input itself; it reads as a large grey square.
- `tools/validate_resume.py` and `tests/journey-resume.spec.ts` deliberately require the start card's own Auto-fill and
  Upload buttons (first-timers never found Upload), so the duplication above is a recorded decision, left as is.
