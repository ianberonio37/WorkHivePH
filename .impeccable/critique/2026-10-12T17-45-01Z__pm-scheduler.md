## Design Specificity Verdict
A PM board that knows its trade: SMRP compliance over 90 days, a stair the hive has to clear, per-asset scope items with
their own frequencies, vehicle-specific task lists (walk-around, tyres, registration and insurance renewals). The words
are a fleet's; the defects were in what the bars and dates claimed.

Deterministic scan (Assessment B): detector clean - no claim needed. Walk: 0 overlap, 0 controls under 44 px, 0
low-contrast readings, smallest text 12 px, 0 heading skips, no spill, at both widths; theme 10 of 10 regions on the
tokens (308 reads, 0 off) after the fix.

## What's Working
- The three tiles agree with each other and with the banner: 2 overdue, 0 due soon, 0 of 8 on track, "PM compliance is
  in the red", with the stair the hive must clear named.
- One next action, sized to the problem: "Clear 2 overdue PMs ->".
- Failure is honest: a read that fails says so and offers a Retry that rebuilds every region, not just the list.

## Priority Issues (fixed in this walk, verified live)
- [P1] **A full green bar on every jeepney with no PM plan.** An asset with no tasks got a full bar and, its status
  being "no data", fell through to green: six "all on track" pictures under the tile saying none of the eight is. The
  track now stays empty and grey for no data, and the words beside it carry the state. Verified: six empty grey tracks.
- [P2] **"Next: Sep 4, 2026" on an overdue asset.** The card's line names the earliest due date, which on an overdue
  asset is in the past. The task's own status (the view's is_overdue) now picks the verb: "Overdue since Sep 4, 2026".
- [P2] **The day count used the device's midnight.** "Due today / Due tomorrow / 3d overdue" cut both dates at the
  device's local midnight. Measured on a throwaway page with a London device clock at 18:43 on 12 October (Manila
  already 13 October): a task due today read "Due tomorrow", yesterday's read "Due today", tomorrow's read "Due in 2d".
  Both ends now use Manila's calendar - the same one whFmtDate already prints the date in. Correct in London, Los
  Angeles, Auckland and Manila.
- [P2] **The card edge was 4px of status colour** - now 1px, as DESIGN.md rules and inventory's part cards already are.
- [P3] **Tints on the wrong palette**: 11 red, 14 amber, 13 green and the grey-400 neutrals moved onto the tokens'
  channels (red #f87171, orange #F7A21B, green #4ade80, white alphas for "no data").
- [P3] **A stored label in the device's locale.** Completing a PM linked to an active project wrote "PM <asset>:
  10/13/2026" or "13/10/2026" into project_links, depending on the completing phone; other members read it in Project
  Manager. It is now written with the platform's Manila date formatter.
