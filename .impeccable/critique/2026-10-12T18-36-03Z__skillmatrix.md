## Design Specificity Verdict
A competency ladder written for maintenance trades - five levels per discipline, TESDA-style progression, a radar of
target against actual, a quiz that unlocks the next level. Its sentences had drifted from its own screen.

Deterministic scan (Assessment B): detector clean - no claim needed. Walk: 0 overlap, 0 controls under 44 px (1 before:
the inline "Resume" link, 62x18), 0 low-contrast readings, smallest text 12 px, 0 heading skips, 0 layout motion, no
spill, at both widths; theme 10 of 11 readers on the tokens - the 11th is Electrical's categorical gold. The 35 "nested
cards" are each card's level pill and five level dots (bordered and rounded, so the measure counts them), not cards.

## What's Working
- The growth path is stated in one line: pass a quiz, earn the badge, it lands on your resume.
- Privacy is explicit: skills, badges and attempts are visible only to the person.
- Every stepper names its discipline and value for a screen reader.
