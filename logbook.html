A finished entry reads like this: Compressor C-201 · Breakdown · bearing noise at the drive end → replaced bearing 6205 and re-greased · 1.5 h down · Closed.

How this page works
  • Three steps: pick the asset and what kind of work it was, describe the problem and what you did, then save. Your typing is kept as you go, so an interruption does not lose it.
  • Who sees it: entries are shared with your hive - your team and supervisor read them, and they feed PM compliance and the asset's history.
  • Open vs Closed: Closed means the job is finished. Open leaves it on your open-jobs list and on the team board until someone closes it.
  • Offline: if you lose signal mid-entry the save is held on this device and syncs when you reconnect - it tells you when that happens.
  • Parts and photos: adding a part deducts it from Inventory; a photo can be read by the AI to draft the entry, which you can edit before saving.
