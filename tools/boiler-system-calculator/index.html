Boiler System Sizing Calculator

The Boiler System Sizing Calculator finds the boiler capacity (kW/BHP) and fuel consumption from steam demand, pressure, and feedwater temperature. Example: for a steam boiler delivering 500 kg/h at 7 barg from 80 °C feedwater on diesel, Boiler capacity = 422.5 kW, Boiler HP = 43.1 BHP, Fuel use = 49.9 L/h (per ASME BPVC Sec.I/IV | ASME B31.1).
