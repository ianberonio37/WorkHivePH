Short Circuit Calculator
**The Short Circuit Calculator finds the prospective fault current (kA) and peak at a board from the transformer and cable impedance. Example: for a 1000 kVA 5%-impedance transformer feeding a 400 V board via 30 m of 240 mm² cable, 3-phase fault = 21.05 kA, Peak = 39.22 kA, Fault level = 14.58 MVA (per IEC 60909-0:2016 | PEC 2017 Art.2.40 | IEEE 141 | IEEE 242).**
