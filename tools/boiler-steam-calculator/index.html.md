> The Boiler / Steam Duty Calculator finds the boiler heat duty (kW and boiler HP) from steam pressure, flow, and feedwater temperature. Free online, with a worked example. Built for Philippine plants by WorkHive.

Source: https://workhiveph.com/tools/boiler-steam-calculator/

**The Boiler / Steam Duty Calculator finds the boiler heat duty (kW and boiler HP) from steam pressure, flow, and feedwater temperature. Example: for 1 kg/s of saturated steam at 10 bar from 80 °C feedwater, Boiler duty = 2441.32 kW, Boiler HP = 248.9 BHP, Saturation temp = 179.9 °C (per ASME BPVC Sec.I/IV | ASME B31.1 | ASME PTC 4 | IAPWS-IF97 | RA 8407 (PH)).**
