Power Factor Correction Calculator
**The Power Factor Correction Calculator sizes the capacitor bank (kVAR) needed to raise power factor from its present value to a target. Example: for a 100 kW load improving power factor from 0.80 to 0.95, kVAR required = 42.13 kVAR, Selected bank = 50 kVAR, Per phase = 16.67 kVAR (per IEEE 18-2012 | IEEE 1036-2010).**
