HVAC Cooling Load Calculator

The HVAC Cooling Load Calculator estimates the cooling load (kW and tons of refrigeration) for a space from area, occupancy, equipment, and climate. Example: for a 150 m² space with 25 people, 15 kW of equipment, at 35 °C outdoor / 24 °C indoor, Cooling load = 38.66 kW, Tons = 10.99 TR, Recommended unit = 14.22 TR (per ASHRAE 62.1 | ASHRAE 90.1 | ASHRAE 55 | ASHRAE 2021 Ch.18).
