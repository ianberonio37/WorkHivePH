# Privacy Policy

> How WorkHive collects, uses, and protects your data. Compliant with the Philippine Data Privacy Act. No selling, no advertising tracking, no dark patterns.

Source: https://workhiveph.com/privacy-policy/

Privacy

Last updated 10 Nov 2026

This page explains what data WorkHive collects, how we use it, and your rights. Plain language; no dark patterns.

## In short

- We collect only what WorkHive needs to work.
- We never sell your data or use it for ads.
- Your hive's data stays in your hive unless you agree.
- You can ask to see, export, fix or delete your data.

## What we collect

WorkHive collects only the data needed to operate the platform:

- **Email address** when you sign up for early access or create an account.
- **Account profile** (display name, role, hive membership) you provide during sign-up.
- **Operational data you enter** in your hive. That covers logbook entries, PM tasks done, asset records and skill matrix entries. It also means voice journal text and fault notes.
- **Tech data**: time stamps, your IP address for security checks, your browser type, and page views.

We do not collect government IDs, card or bank details, or location data. We collect nothing else that WorkHive does not need to work. WorkHive does not process payments: no money moves through the platform.

**One exception, and it is worth naming.** On the Marketplace, a provider can buy service credits. A provider can also settle a job there. Those payments happen in GCash, between people, outside WorkHive.

When one happens, we record only what we need to check it. That is the amount, the method and the GCash reference number you file.

This is the only data on WorkHive that touches a payment. We keep it so anyone can check a top-up or a payment against what really arrived. None of it is a card, an account number or a bank login.

The Marketplace is optional. Every maintenance tool on WorkHive is free and records nothing like this.

## How we use it

- To create your account and scope your access to the hives you have joined.
- To run the tools you use, from the Logbook to the AI Assistant.
- To send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset, hive invitations).
- To improve the platform through aggregated, anonymized usage analytics.

We never sell your data. We never use your operational data for advertising. We never give your hive data to another hive unless you clearly agree.

## AI Work Assistant and your data

The AI Work Assistant operates on your hive data to answer your questions. Inputs and outputs are scoped to your hive. Our AI providers run large language models for us. Our contracts forbid them to train their general models on your plant data.

The Voice Journal does not keep your voice recordings at all. It turns the audio into text and deletes the audio right away. Only the text goes into your account. ## Data sharing

We share data only with:

- **Members of your hive**: they see what the roles you and your supervisor set up allow.
- **Service providers** who run parts of WorkHive for us. Their contracts bind them to keep your data private. They are Supabase (database), our AI providers and our email senders.
- **Law enforcement**, only when the law compels us. That means a valid Philippine subpoena or court order.

## Your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data on the platform.
- **Export** your data in a portable format.
- **Correct** inaccurate data.
- **Delete** your account and all associated personal data.
- **Object** to specific uses of your data.
- **Complain** to the National Privacy Commission if you think we broke these rights.

To use any of these rights, email [admin@workhiveph.com](mailto:admin@workhiveph.com). Put "Data Rights Request" in the subject line.

## Cookies and analytics

WorkHive uses essential cookies to keep you signed in. On public pages, Google Analytics 4 counts visits in total. It counts page views and actions such as opening or sending the sign-up form.

Analytics hides your IP address. We never send it names, emails, phone numbers or any of your work records. We do not use third-party ad cookies. Inside the app, we log usage in our own database, not in Google. The sections above cover that data too.

## Data retention

We keep account and hive data for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we erase your personal data within 30 days. We may keep totals that name no one, with no end date.

Some records stay after you delete your account. These are compliance records, like DOLE OSHS and ISO audit logs. Our audit-log policy keeps them for 10 years. Records of incident investigations stay for 15 years. This follows what the regulations require.

## Changes to this policy

We will tell you at least 30 days before any change that matters takes effect. We will post the notice on the platform and at [workhiveph.com/learn/](https://workhiveph.com/learn/). The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest change.
