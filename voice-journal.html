How this works

Record or type a note. The assistant turns it into a structured entry and replies. Your note is saved as soon as the assistant replies — there is no separate save step, and an entry cannot be edited or removed here afterwards, so leave out anything you would not want kept. To have your personal data erased, send a Data Rights Request to admin@workhiveph.com. Use the voice buttons above to control spoken replies. Your past entries stay in the list below.

Your voice recording is not kept: the audio is transcribed and then discarded, and only the text of your entry is saved to your account. Speak in Filipino, English or Taglish — the transcript keeps whatever you actually said.
