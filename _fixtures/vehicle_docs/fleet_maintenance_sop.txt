Fleet Vehicle Preventive Maintenance SOP
This standard operating procedure governs preventive maintenance for the company van fleet.
Service Intervals
Replace engine oil and oil filter every 10,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first.
Rotate tires every 10,000 km to equalise wear.
Replace the air filter element every 20,000 km.
Replace brake fluid every 40,000 km or 24 months.
Replace engine coolant every 100,000 km.
The first service after purchase is due at 1,000 km (one-time break-in check).
