How sending works
  • Reports selected: chips you've tapped to include (PM Overdue, Failure Digest, Shift Handover, Predictive, OEE, Weekly Analytics).
  • Recipients: saved contacts you've ticked + any one-time email typed below.
  • Saved contacts: contacts persisted to your hive, reusable across sends.
  • Verdict: green when at least one report and one recipient are picked; empty otherwise.

Voice context is optional: tap the mic to dictate a custom note that gets pre-pended to the email body.
