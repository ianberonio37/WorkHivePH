Which browsers WorkHive supports

WorkHive needs a 2020-or-newer browser engine (Chrome/Edge 80+, Safari 14+, Firefox 74+, or Android System WebView updated since 2020). On an older engine the app cannot run, and it says so with a banner instead of showing a blank page - if you see that banner, update Chrome or Android System WebView from the Play Store.

On a supported browser, everything else here degrades gracefully: offline entries queue and sync, and failed reads say what failed rather than showing empty screens.
