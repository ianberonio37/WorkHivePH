How this page works
  • This is the platform's own cockpit, not a hive board: the numbers here span every hive, not just yours.
  • Four tabs. Operations holds what waits for you. Platform shows how WorkHive is doing. Feeds says whether it is up. Developer shows what the builder watches.
  • Anything that changes the platform is confirm-gated. Read the sentence in the confirm before pressing it, because most of these actions reach other people's hives.
  • A figure with no source chip beside it has not been re-measured this session.
  • A day on this console is a Manila day (UTC+8): "today" starts at midnight in Manila, not on your device's clock.

Listings awaiting review

Approve to publish to buyers, or reject
-

Approve publishes the listing to every buyer immediately. You can still remove it later. Reject asks you for a reason, and the seller sees that reason and can fix and resubmit. A listing goes live only when its seller holds credits worth 10% of the price. So a refusal here may be about their balance, not your decision.

Find a published listing

Take a live listing back to review
-

Search by title, seller, or the listing id a buyer's report names. Unpublish takes the listing off the marketplace and puts it back in review. The seller sees your reason and can fix and resubmit. Approving it again follows the usual rules, so a new seller already over the three-live-listing limit stays in review until their first sale.
