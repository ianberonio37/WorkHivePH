## DOE Designated Establishment Threshold

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the purpose of RA 11285?

The purpose of RA 11285 is to promote energy efficiency and conservation in the Philippines. The law aims to reduce energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions.

### Who is responsible for complying with RA 11285?

Establishments that consume 500,000 kWh a year or more are required to comply with RA 11285 - Type 1 from 500,000 kWh to under 4 million kWh, Type 2 at 4 million kWh and above. This includes industrial, commercial, and residential establishments.

### What is the deadline for submitting the annual energy report?

Section 20(f) of RA 11285 sets it plainly: a designated establishment submits its annual Energy Consumption and Conservation Report (ECCR) to the DOE by the 15th of April every year.

### What are the penalties for non-compliance with RA 11285?

Section 32 of RA 11285 sets the band at PHP 10,000 to PHP 1,000,000, with the schedule inside that band fixed by the DOE and the implementing rules and rising for repeat offences. Section 33 is the one worth knowing about separately: for a wilful violation a responsible officer faces imprisonment of one to five years, or a fine of PHP 100,000 to PHP 100,000,000. Read the current schedule from the DOE rather than from a summary.
