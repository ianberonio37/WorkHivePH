## What a free CMMS tier usually caps

Read a free tier by what it *caps*, not what it advertises. The usual limits are users, assets or work orders, and how much history you keep. Some also leave out reports or PM automation entirely. Those caps are on purpose. The free tier is the top of a sales funnel. The cap sits exactly where a growing plant will hit it.

That is not dishonest. It is a business model, and for a funded plant the paid product may well be worth it. But "free CMMS" and "free forever for my whole crew" are different questions. CMMS means computerized maintenance management system. Ask three things. How many users? How many assets? And what happens to my history if I stop paying?

## The Philippine-specific criteria most lists ignore

International comparison lists rank on features. For a Philippine plant, four practical tests usually decide if the tool gets used:

- **Offline capture.** If the tool needs signal at the asset, entries get written from memory later, or not at all.
- **Language.** Techs who work in Filipino or Taglish record more in their own language. They record it more accurately, too.
- **Local compliance.** An inspector asks for DOLE OSHS audit trails and LOTO permits. LOTO means lock-out/tag-out. They also ask for RA 11285 energy reports. See the [compliance guide](https://workhiveph.com/learn/ph-plant-compliance-guide/).
- **Cost in pesos per seat.** $20 to $25 per user per month weighs more in a Philippine plant than in a US one. A flat-fee plan changes the maths again once the crew grows.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free CMMS software in 2026?

The free plans still on offer in October 2026 are Coast, MaintainX, Fiix and Maintenance Care: all freemium, meaning the free plan is capped (repeating work orders, users, assets or history) and designed to convert to paid seats. Limble no longer lists a free plan. WorkHive is free at the worker tier as its actual model, and adds offline capture, Filipino-language entry, and Philippine compliance records.

### Is there a CMMS that is free forever?

WorkHive is free at the worker tier with no per-seat cost. Most other free options are freemium tiers with caps on users, assets, or history that are designed to be outgrown.

### What is the cheapest CMMS for a small factory?

Published entry pricing for the main per-seat products runs from $20 to $25 per user per month billed yearly (MaintainX $20, UpKeep $24, Coast $25), with Fiix at $45 and Tractian at $60; Limble and eMaint quote on request. Maintenance Care is the exception: a flat $250 a month with unlimited users. On per-seat pricing the total cost is driven by seat count, which is why a genuinely free worker tier changes the maths.

### Do free CMMS tools work offline?

Most do not: they assume connectivity and degrade on a plant floor with dead spots. WorkHive is offline-first: entries are captured locally at the asset and sync when a connection returns.

### Should a small plant use a spreadsheet instead?

Spreadsheets work until they do not: formulas drift, one person owns the file, and history is lost when they leave. Since capable free options exist, the spreadsheet is rarely the cheapest choice once you count the lost history.
