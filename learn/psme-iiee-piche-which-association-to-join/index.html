PSME, IIEE, PIChE: Which Philippine Engineering Association?

By WorkHive Editorial Team · 18 May 2026 · 10 min read
Short answer: Pick the association that matches your discipline and has a chapter that meets near you. PSME is for mechanical engineers. IIEE is for electrical, and PIChE for chemical. No association sets your CPD quota. Under RA 10912, the PRC sets it through the Board for your discipline. Your Professional Identification Card runs for 3 years. An association is where you earn the units. So choose one whose events you can actually attend.

Overview of Philippine Engineering Associations

Three associations cover most plant engineers. PSME is the Philippine Society of Mechanical Engineers. IIEE is the Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines. PIChE is the Philippine Institute of Chemical Engineers. Each serves its own discipline.

AFEO links national engineering bodies across ASEAN. AFEO is the ASEAN Federation of Engineering Organisations.

Keep licensure separate from membership. The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) runs the board exams. It also registers Professional Mechanical and Professional Electrical Engineers. The associations run review classes, their own certifications and CPD-accredited training.

Membership Cost and Benefits

Dues differ by association and by membership class. The classes are student, associate, full and life. They also change from year to year. So trust only each association's own current fee schedule. Ignore numbers quoted second-hand.

Compare like with like. Check the annual dues, the joining fee and the membership class. Many employers pay for one membership, so ask yours before you choose.

Dues buy access. You get CPD-accredited events, technical talks and publications. You also get a network of peers. Members usually pay less for events than non-members.

Budget for two costs, not one. There is the event fee, and there are the leave days to attend. On rotating shifts, the leave days are often the harder cost. A supervisor has to plan around them, not just approve them.

CPD Credits and Exam Pathways

CPD means continuing professional development. RA 10912 is the CPD Act of 2016. Under it, you need CPD units to renew your PRC card. The PRC sets the number. Each discipline's Professional Regulatory Board decides it. PRC Resolution No. 2019-1146 took effect on 1 March 2019. It cut the units and widened the activities that earn them.

So read the current figure from your own Board. Then pick the association whose calendar makes that easy. PSME, IIEE and PIChE all run CPD-accredited activities, but none of them sets the quota.

Keep exams separate too. The PRC and its Boards run the licensure exams. They also run the professional registrations above them. An association's own certification is a professional credential, not a licence. Ask which of its programmes is a certification and which is review for a PRC exam. The two often share a page.

If you work far from Metro Manila, count the travel and leave days too. They are often bigger than the course fee.

Networking Events and Community

Each association holds national conventions, seminars and workshops. Most also run local chapter meetings. A chapter near your plant beats a bigger body that meets only in Manila.

Shift work makes events hard to attend. Many associations now run webinars and online sessions. Check their times against your roster before you pay.

Ask a member before you join. What did they get for last year's dues? Did the CPD sessions fit a shift worker's hours? Does the local chapter actually meet?

Frequently asked questions

What are the benefits of joining a Philippine engineering association?
Joining a Philippine engineering association provides opportunities for professional development, networking, and access to industry resources. For example, PSME members can access CPD credits and technical publications.
How do I earn CPD credits as a Filipino engineer?
Filipino engineers can earn CPD credits through attending seminars, workshops, and conferences organized by their chosen association. For instance, IIEE offers CPD credits for attending its annual convention.
What is the difference between PSME and IIEE?
PSME (Philippine Society of Mechanical Engineers) focuses on mechanical engineering, while IIEE (Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers) focuses on electrical engineering. Both associations offer membership benefits and CPD credits.
Can I join multiple engineering associations?
Yes. Each has its own requirements and dues, and most offer a student rate. Many engineers keep one main membership and attend other bodies' open events.
How do I stay updated on industry developments and trends?
Attend your association's conferences, seminars and webinars. You can also ask peers in the WorkHive Community, a forum for your hive and, if you choose, the plant-wide feed.
What is the role of ASEAN Federation in Philippine engineering associations?
The ASEAN Federation of Engineering Organisations (AFEO) links national engineering bodies across ASEAN. It supports regional cooperation between engineers.
