## What the AI Companion is

The AI Companion is the chat layer that follows you across WorkHive. It sits on the floating launcher on every page, and you can talk to it through the Voice Journal. A single-page chatbot forgets you when you leave. This one carries your context with you. It also gives two expert views on the same problem. You choose the persona that fits the job: the technical expert or the strategist.

Both personas run on the same language models and the same memory. But each draws on **a different slice of a curated domain knowledge base**. That is why Hezekiah sounds like a reliability engineer. Zaniah sounds like a maintenance manager. They differ even when you ask them the same question.

## Hezekiah, the technical expert

Hezekiah is the hands-on reliability and maintenance expert. He draws on the technical knowledge. That covers failure modes, FMEA and vibration. It covers condition monitoring, lubrication and alignment. It also covers PM task design and the standards behind it. Ask Hezekiah when the question has a right answer grounded in physics or a standard:

- "What broadband vibration velocity means I should take a machine offline?"
- "What goes on a monthly PM checklist for a centrifugal pump?"
- "Walk me through an FMEA for a bottling line filler."
- "How do MTBF (mean time between failures) and MTTR (mean time to repair) differ? Give me the formulas."

He answers like a senior technician who has read every standard. His answers are specific, sourced and practical.

## Zaniah, the strategist

Zaniah is the maintenance strategist. She draws on the strategic knowledge. That covers OEE and reliability benchmarks, and which KPI to pick. OEE is overall equipment effectiveness. A KPI is a key performance indicator. It also covers maturity models and the cost of downtime. And it covers life-cycle cost and what to do first. Ask Zaniah when the question is about *what to do next* and *why it is worth it*:

- "We are reactive today. What is the highest-return next step?"
- "Which two KPIs should I put on the plant dashboard first?"
- "What OEE should we target and how far are we from it?"
- "How do I justify a predictive-maintenance investment to management?"

She answers like a reliability manager who plans the climb. You spend on the next rung, not two rungs up.

> Same question, two answers. Ask both how to improve OEE. Hezekiah gives the technical levers. Those are availability, performance and quality losses. Zaniah gives the order to work in. Measure first, fix the biggest loss, then benchmark. Use both.

## How it grounds its answers (two planes)

The companion never answers from thin air. It fuses two planes of knowledge at the moment you ask:

- **The global brain.** A curated base of maintenance and reliability know-how. It also holds the standard definitions of MTBF, MTTR and OEE. This is the same for every customer and contains *no* live plant data.
- **Your hive's private context.** Your logbook, your asset list, your PM schedule and your skill matrix. It stays inside your hive and only feeds answers for your own workers.

Hezekiah draws on the technical slice of the global brain. Zaniah draws on the strategic slice. Both can use the shared basics, like safety and definitions. Then either persona adds your private context, so the answer fits your plant. The two planes stay apart. General know-how lives in one and your data in the other.

**Why two planes matter:** it is what lets the companion be both deep and safe. The global brain makes it an expert on day one, before your hive has much history. Your private context makes the answer about *your* equipment. Your data never enters the shared brain. So one customer's data can never show up in another's answer.

## What it is NOT good at

- **Safety decisions.** LOTO (lock-out/tag-out) scope, permit issuance, confined-space entry. Always human-signed, never delegated to the companion.
- **Overriding the OEM manual or DOLE OSHS.** If the companion disagrees with the maker's manual or the law, those win.
- **Management calls about people, budgets, and vendors.** Zaniah can frame the trade-off; the decision stays human.
- **Real-time sensor interpretation.** It reasons over history and standards, not a live orbit plot on a turbine.

## How to get the best answer

1. **Pick the persona by the job.** Technical call → Hezekiah. Strategy call → Zaniah.
2. **Name the asset or the goal.** Hezekiah wants the asset and the symptom. Zaniah wants the business goal and today's numbers.
3. **Let it pull your hive's context.** The more you fill in your logbook and assets, the sharper the answer.
4. **Verify against the source.** The companion cites its sources. Check them before you act on a safety or compliance call.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who are Hezekiah and Zaniah in the WorkHive AI Companion?

They are the two personas of the AI Companion. Hezekiah is the technical reliability expert: failure modes, vibration and condition monitoring, PM tasks, standards, diagnostics. Zaniah is the maintenance strategist: OEE and reliability benchmarks, KPI choice, maturity steps, cost-of-downtime, where to invest next. Same companion, two lenses.

### How is the AI Companion different from the AI Work Assistant?

The AI Work Assistant is the chat on the assistant page, focused on plant-floor fault diagnosis from your hive data. The AI Companion is the cross-page, voice-capable layer with the Hezekiah and Zaniah personas, and it adds a curated DOMAIN knowledge base of authoritative expertise on top of your hive data. They share the same models and memory.

### Where does the AI Companion get its knowledge?

Two planes, fused at answer time. A global persona knowledge base of curated expertise plus standard definitions (MTBF, MTTR, OEE), the same for every customer; and your hive's private context (logbook, assets, PM, skills), isolated to your hive. The companion retrieves a persona-scoped slice of the first and fuses it with the second.

### Does the companion mix up one customer's data with another's?

No. The global persona knowledge base holds only general expertise, no customer's live data ever enters it. Your hive's private data stays isolated to your hive and is only ever fused into answers for your own workers. The two planes are kept strictly separate by design.

### Can I talk to the AI Companion in Filipino or Taglish, and by voice?

Yes. It accepts English, Filipino and Taglish and replies in the language you used, and through the Voice Journal it also handles Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Kapampangan, Waray, Bikol and Pangasinan, and it supports voice through the Voice Journal: speak your entry or question and it transcribes and responds, which is faster than typing with gloves on.
