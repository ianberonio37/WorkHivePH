## Where MaintainX is the better choice than WorkHive

- **In-app communication.** MaintainX puts the chat on the work order itself. The job and the talk about it never drift apart. If your team works chat-first, that is a real advantage.
- **Native mobile polish.** A native app beats a browser PWA (a web app you install) for heavy daily use.
- **Procurement comfort.** Some firms find a funded vendor with support deals easier to defend.
- **Free requester seats.** On every MaintainX tier, staff who only send work requests are free, with no cap. So a plant with many requesters and few techs pays less than the per-seat price suggests.

## Where WorkHive is the better choice than MaintainX

- **No per-seat cost.** Free at the worker tier means more technicians cost nothing more. That is the opposite of per-seat pricing.
- **Dead-spot capture.** Offline-first works in dead spots. You write the record at the asset, not from memory hours later.
- **Filipino-language capture** including Taglish voice-to-text.
- ****Philippine compliance** records. That means the DOLE OSHS audit trail and LOTO (lock-out/tag-out) permits. It also means the action record an RA 11285 energy audit asks for. WorkHive logs the energy-related actions, not the kilowatt-hours. Plus 60 engineering calculators for the design side.**

## Frequently asked questions

### Is MaintainX free?

Partly. MaintainX's Basic plan is free forever with unlimited work orders and unlimited requester users, but it holds only 2 active repeating work orders and 2 work orders with attached procedures, with one month of advanced analytics. Paid plans start at $20 per user per month billed yearly ($25 monthly), and Premium is $65 yearly ($75 monthly), as published on 27 October 2026. A preventive-maintenance schedule outgrows two repeating work orders quickly, which is where the free plan is designed to convert to paid seats.

### What is the main difference between WorkHive and MaintainX?

MaintainX is a mobile-first work-order platform with strong in-app messaging on a per-seat commercial model. WorkHive is free at the worker tier and offline-first, built specifically for Philippine plants with Filipino-language capture and local compliance records.

### Which is better for a plant with bad wifi?

WorkHive, because it is offline-first: capture happens locally at the asset and syncs later. Connectivity-oriented apps lose entries or push technicians to record from memory once they are back in signal.

### Does WorkHive have a mobile app?

WorkHive is a browser-based progressive web app that installs to the home screen and works offline, so there is no app-store dependency or per-device install friction.
